DLI: ECHOES

Every failure becomes part of the level.

How to Play

Guide Dli to the glowing portal in every room. Every time you fail, you leave behind a solid crystal Echo of yourself. Echoes stay in the level, so your failures become the tools that solve the puzzle.

What Echoes can do

  • Stand on them. An Echo is a solid 1.2 m block. Use it as a step to reach ledges you can't jump to.
  • Hold pressure plates. Doors open while every plate with their letter and color is pressed.
  • Block lasers. A laser curtain stops at the first Echo in its path.
  • Cover spikes. Echoes don't mind spikes, so walk across on top of them.
  • Stack them. Leave an Echo while standing on one to build higher.

Level elements

  • Pressure plate + door: same letter and color. The door stays open only while its plates are held.
  • Quiz terminal: walk up and press E. The right answer unlocks the matching door for good. A wrong answer zaps you, which leaves an Echo.
  • Spikes: touching them kills you.
  • Laser curtain: deadly and too tall to jump over. Block it with an Echo.
  • Ledges: you can jump onto 1 m blocks. Anything higher needs an Echo.
  • Checkpoint: step on the ring to respawn there.
  • Portal: reach it to finish the level.

Rules

  • Each level has an Echo limit. Leaving one more makes your oldest Echo shatter.
  • Falling into the void leaves no Echo.
  • Finish with deaths at or under the level's par for ⭐⭐⭐.
